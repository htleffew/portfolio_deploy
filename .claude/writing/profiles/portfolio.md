# Profile: PORTFOLIO (GitHub Pages site)

Applies to prose on the portfolio site (`Pm_html` → `portfolio_deploy` /
`portfolio_astro`): case studies, method writeups, project pages. Obey **CORE**
first, then everything here.

Canonical worked specimen: `design_system/ui_kits/case-study/clinical-validation.html`.
Where this profile and that page disagree, the page is right and this file is fixed
to match.

## Who is writing

Dr. Heather Leffew: applied researcher in machine learning, computational
psychology, and high-stakes prediction systems (clinical neurotherapy, forensic
and threat assessment, public-safety analytics, enterprise ML at TikTok USDS,
founding the data-science function at Spokeo). The voice is precise, direct, and
argument-forward. It does not perform expertise; it makes a case. When a piece is
dense it is dense because the argument is dense, not because the prose is padded.

## Two page types, two sources of concreteness (the load-bearing rule)

- **Research pages** — her own studies and independent analyses (the manifesto
  power study, the dissertation, the Claude behavior analyses, TAT/autism work).
  The data is hers to publish, so concreteness comes from real numbers: t-values,
  corpus counts, exact rates. These read as authentic already; they need voice
  polish, not new material.
- **Approach pages** — general explainers, walk-throughs, tutorials about methods
  adjacent to current or recent employment (evals essays, safety-audit and
  agentic-evaluation pieces). These MUST NOT carry proprietary metrics, employer
  findings, or any specific personal achievement. Concreteness comes instead from a
  worked illustrative example (an attack walked turn by turn), precisely named
  mechanisms (a natural-language-inference check on the criminal-record sentence,
  not "an advanced filter"), the actual design tradeoffs, and the honest limits of
  the approach.

Trigger: whose work is it. From a job → teach the generalizable method, no
proprietary specifics. Heather's own independent research → be specific, share the
real methods and findings.

## What the authentic pages do

1. **Lead with the specific, not the thesis.** Open on the number and the method,
   not on why measurement matters.
2. **Put real numbers where the claim lives** (research pages only).
3. **Admit the unglamorous bookkeeping** (the one uncategorized document; why a
   validator fires at 0.95).
4. **Pre-empt the objection a reviewer would raise**, in line.
5. **Name the provenance and the arc** — place the work in her own line of work.

## Pairing with the visual system

The portfolio runs the "Obsidian & Chrome" design system (`STYLE.md`): sharp
zero-radii, dual mode, institutional. Prose density and restraint should match that
gravity; no SaaS cheerfulness.

## Exit checklist (gate, not method)

Run after drafting, before publish:

- [ ] CORE clean (no banned tokens, no dashes, no negative parallelism, no canned openers)
- [ ] Correct page type identified; approach pages carry zero proprietary specifics
- [ ] Opens on a specific, not a thesis
- [ ] Every major claim has a number, mechanism, or named instance
- [ ] At least one piece of bookkeeping or pre-empted objection
- [ ] Provenance/arc named
